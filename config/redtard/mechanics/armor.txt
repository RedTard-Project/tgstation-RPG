# Armor datums. The id is the path after /datum/rogue_armor/; items point at it with armor = id. Ratings are tiers, as in Ratwood and Azure Peak: blunt absorbs 0-5, slash, stab and piercing block 0-4 against the penetration tier of an intent, fire and acid reduce 0-5.
# Fields: blunt, slash, stab, piercing, fire, acid, magic
